Where the recommendations come from. Fleischner Society 2017 guidance for incidental pulmonary nodules, ACR Lung-RADS v2022 for screening scans, and SCCT/STR guidance on coronary calcium reported on non-gated chest CT.

Prototype. Explanations run on this device for demonstration. Sign-in, reminders, the second-read service and payments are not live. A production version would run on HIPAA-ready infrastructure under a signed BAA.
